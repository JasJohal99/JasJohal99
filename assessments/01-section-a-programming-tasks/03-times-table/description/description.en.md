> **Marks:** 10
{: .callout-info}

Write a program that:

- asks the user for a number `n`,
- prints the times table for `n` from `1` up to and including `12`, one line per multiplication, in the format `n × i = product`.

Use the multiplication-sign character `×` (U+00D7) — not a lowercase letter `x`.

## Example interaction

```console
Enter a number: 7
7 × 1 = 7
7 × 2 = 14
7 × 3 = 21
7 × 4 = 28
7 × 5 = 35
7 × 6 = 42
7 × 7 = 49
7 × 8 = 56
7 × 9 = 63
7 × 10 = 70
7 × 11 = 77
7 × 12 = 84
```
