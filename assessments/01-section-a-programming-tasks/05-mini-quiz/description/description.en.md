> **Marks:** 10
{: .callout-info}

Write a short quiz with three questions. For each question:

- ask the question,
- read the user's answer,
- if the answer is correct, increase a `score` variable by `1`.

When all three questions have been answered, print `You scored N out of 3` (with `N` replaced by the final score).

The three questions and their expected answers are:

1. `What is 5 + 7? ` → `12`
2. `What is the capital of France? ` → `Paris` (case-sensitive)
3. `What colour do you get when you mix red and blue? ` → `purple` (accept any capitalisation, e.g. `Purple` or `PURPLE`)

## Example interactions

```console
What is 5 + 7? 12
What is the capital of France? Paris
What colour do you get when you mix red and blue? purple
You scored 3 out of 3
```

```console
What is 5 + 7? 0
What is the capital of France? London
What colour do you get when you mix red and blue? blue
You scored 0 out of 3
```
